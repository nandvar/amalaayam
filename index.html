Amalaayam — Pure Abode

From the Sanskrit amala — pure, spotless, untouched. Amalaayam is a home built on clarity and positivity. A name that carries gentle, uplifting energy — a space where you arrive and simply feel at ease.

The Property

Welcome to अमलायम्

अमलायम् is a luxury villa on a half-acre estate in Badshahpur, Gurgaon. Six bedrooms across a main house and outhouse, each with an attached bathroom — designed for families, groups, and travellers who value privacy, comfort, and elegance.

With two living areas, three patios, a private pool with jacuzzi, an outdoor kitchen, landscaped gardens, and modern interiors throughout — this is not a hotel room. It's an experience.
